### Youth cycling and professional careers

**[ciclismo-giovanile-vs-pro](https://github.com/lucabnt/ciclismo-giovanile-vs-pro)** · Python

Do Italian youth rankings (U15–U23) predict who turns professional, and from what age?
Ten years in the team car, this time with a dataset of 11,000 riders.

Short answer: yes, already at 13. But predicting is not selecting. Keep only the top 10%
of the U19 ranking and you catch 59% of future pros, yet half of the riders you kept
never make it. The ranking is a good tool for deciding who to follow, and a poor one
for deciding who to let go.

[Read the series (Italian)](https://lucabontempi.com/series/ciclismo-giovanile-e-professionismo/)
