### Youth cycling and professional careers

**[ciclismo-giovanile-vs-pro](https://github.com/lucabnt/ciclismo-giovanile-vs-pro)** · Python

Do Italian youth rankings (U15–U23) predict who turns professional, and from what age?
Ten years in the team car, this time with a dataset of 11,000 riders.

Short answer: yes, already at 13. But predicting is not selecting. Keep only the top 10%
of the U19 ranking and you catch 59% of future pros, yet half of the riders you kept
never make it. The ranking is a good tool for deciding who to follow, and a poor one
for deciding who to let go.

[Read the series](https://lucabontempi.com/en/series/ciclismo-giovanile-e-professionismo/)


### Testing young riders, offline

**[performance-lab](https://github.com/lucabnt/performance-lab)** · Python · TypeScript

Fuorigiri Performance Lab is the app I built to record and compare my club's tests:
incremental tests from FIT/CSV or by hand, lactate, sprint and Wingate, training zones,
growth and maturation. It works offline first, with one local archive per club and optional
encrypted sharing between coaches. Windows and Android, Italian and English.

It is a first alpha (0.10.0-alpha.1), so keep a backup.
The shared UI kit and Python runtime live in [cycling-suite-common](https://github.com/lucabnt/cycling-suite-common),
which also serves club apps that are not public yet.

[Download the alpha](https://github.com/lucabnt/performance-lab/releases) · [Project page](https://lucabontempi.com/projects/fuorigiri-performance-lab/)

### Waking a PC from my phone

**[AwakeOnLANMobile](https://github.com/lucabnt/AwakeOnLANMobile)** · Android

An Android Wake-on-LAN app I maintain as a fork of [nellogan's original](https://github.com/nellogan/AwakeOnLANMobile).
Wake a saved PC from the app, a home-screen widget or a Quick Settings tile.
Device storage and backups are encrypted, with no accounts, ads or cloud service.
